## 🎬 The Scene

A Saturday at Jamilah's place. Jason, Riduan and Feifei have come over for lunch, but **Feifei isn't feeling well**. The friends help her find the clinic, cook lunch together, and end up planning a holiday to **Langkawi** 🏝️.

## 🎤 How to Practise

1. **Pick a character.** Each person takes one colour.
2. **Listen first.** Open the [interactive version](https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/mandarin-script/) and press 🔊 next to any line, or 🐢 to hear it slowly.
3. **Practice mode.** Choose *“Practice as…”* in the toolbar and your lines get blurred. Press **▶ Play whole scene** and it pauses on your lines so you can say them yourself.
4. **Swap roles** and go again! 🔁

> 🔊 **Best voice:** open the page in **Microsoft Edge** to get the natural *Xiaoxiao* voice. Chrome and Safari also work.
