## 🎬 The Scene

A Saturday at Jamilah's place. Jason, Riduan and Feifei have come over for lunch, but **Feifei isn't feeling well**. The friends help her find the clinic, cook lunch together, and end up planning a holiday to **Langkawi** 🏝️.

## 📜 The Script


### Act 1 · Feifei Feels Unwell 🤒
<sub>第一幕 · 菲菲不舒服</sub>

💚 ![Riduan](https://img.shields.io/badge/Riduan-%E7%A4%BC%E7%AB%AF-0c9c86?style=flat-square) <sub>#1 · line 1/10</sub>  
**菲菲，你怎么了？你不舒服吗？**  
*Fēifēi, nǐ zěnme le? Nǐ bù shūfu ma?*  
💬 Feifei, what's wrong? Are you unwell?

🧡 ![Lin Feifei](https://img.shields.io/badge/Lin_Feifei-%E6%9E%97%E8%8F%B2%E8%8F%B2-f08c00?style=flat-square) <sub>#2 · line 1/10</sub>  
**我头痛，还有点发烧。**  
*Wǒ tóutòng, hái yǒudiǎn fāshāo.*  
💬 I have a headache, and a bit of a fever.

🩷 ![Jamilah](https://img.shields.io/badge/Jamilah-%E4%BD%B3%E7%B1%B3%E6%8B%89-d6336c?style=flat-square) <sub>#3 · line 1/10</sub>  
**你什么时候开始生病的？**  
*Nǐ shénme shíhou kāishǐ shēngbìng de?*  
💬 When did you start feeling sick?

🧡 ![Lin Feifei](https://img.shields.io/badge/Lin_Feifei-%E6%9E%97%E8%8F%B2%E8%8F%B2-f08c00?style=flat-square) <sub>#4 · line 2/10</sub>  
**三天前就开始了。我每天都很累。**  
*Sān tiān qián jiù kāishǐ le. Wǒ měi tiān dōu hěn lèi.*  
💬 It started three days ago. I've been tired every day.

💙 ![Jason](https://img.shields.io/badge/Jason-%E6%9D%B0%E6%A3%AE-3b5bdb?style=flat-square) <sub>#5 · line 1/10</sub>  
**你应该去看医生。诊所离这里不远。**  
*Nǐ yīnggāi qù kàn yīshēng. Zhěnsuǒ lí zhèlǐ bù yuǎn.*  
💬 You should go and see a doctor. The clinic isn't far from here.

🧡 ![Lin Feifei](https://img.shields.io/badge/Lin_Feifei-%E6%9E%97%E8%8F%B2%E8%8F%B2-f08c00?style=flat-square) <sub>#6 · line 3/10</sub>  
**好的。可是诊所在哪里？我没去过。**  
*Hǎo de. Kěshì zhěnsuǒ zài nǎlǐ? Wǒ méi qùguo.*  
💬 Okay. But where is the clinic? I've never been there.

💚 ![Riduan](https://img.shields.io/badge/Riduan-%E7%A4%BC%E7%AB%AF-0c9c86?style=flat-square) <sub>#7 · line 2/10</sub>  
**一直向前走，到了书店就向左转。**  
*Yìzhí xiàng qián zǒu, dàole shūdiàn jiù xiàng zuǒ zhuǎn.*  
💬 Go straight ahead, then turn left when you reach the bookstore.

💙 ![Jason](https://img.shields.io/badge/Jason-%E6%9D%B0%E6%A3%AE-3b5bdb?style=flat-square) <sub>#8 · line 2/10</sub>  
**诊所就在便利店旁边。**  
*Zhěnsuǒ jiù zài biànlìdiàn pángbiān.*  
💬 The clinic is right beside the convenience store.

🩷 ![Jamilah](https://img.shields.io/badge/Jamilah-%E4%BD%B3%E7%B1%B3%E6%8B%89-d6336c?style=flat-square) <sub>#9 · line 2/10</sub>  
**别担心，我陪你去。**  
*Bié dānxīn, wǒ péi nǐ qù.*  
💬 Don't worry, I'll go with you.

🧡 ![Lin Feifei](https://img.shields.io/badge/Lin_Feifei-%E6%9E%97%E8%8F%B2%E8%8F%B2-f08c00?style=flat-square) <sub>#10 · line 4/10</sub>  
**谢谢你，佳米拉！**  
*Xièxie nǐ, Jiāmǐlā!*  
💬 Thank you, Jamilah!

🩷 ![Jamilah](https://img.shields.io/badge/Jamilah-%E4%BD%B3%E7%B1%B3%E6%8B%89-d6336c?style=flat-square) <sub>#11 · line 3/10</sub>  
**不谢。我们是好朋友嘛！**  
*Búxiè. Wǒmen shì hǎo péngyou ma!*  
💬 Don't mention it. We're best friends, after all!


### Act 2 · Cooking Together 🍳
<sub>第二幕 · 一起做饭</sub>

💚 ![Riduan](https://img.shields.io/badge/Riduan-%E7%A4%BC%E7%AB%AF-0c9c86?style=flat-square) <sub>#12 · line 3/10</sub>  
**菲菲，你要不要喝水？**  
*Fēifēi, nǐ yào búyào hē shuǐ?*  
💬 Feifei, do you want some water?

🧡 ![Lin Feifei](https://img.shields.io/badge/Lin_Feifei-%E6%9E%97%E8%8F%B2%E8%8F%B2-f08c00?style=flat-square) <sub>#13 · line 5/10</sub>  
**要，谢谢。我还没有吃午餐呢。**  
*Yào, xièxie. Wǒ hái méiyǒu chī wǔcān ne.*  
💬 Yes, thanks. I haven't even had lunch yet.

💙 ![Jason](https://img.shields.io/badge/Jason-%E6%9D%B0%E6%A3%AE-3b5bdb?style=flat-square) <sub>#14 · line 3/10</sub>  
**我刚从超市回来，买了鸡肉和蔬菜。**  
*Wǒ gāng cóng chāoshì huílái, mǎile jīròu hé shūcài.*  
💬 I just got back from the supermarket — I bought chicken and vegetables.

🩷 ![Jamilah](https://img.shields.io/badge/Jamilah-%E4%BD%B3%E7%B1%B3%E6%8B%89-d6336c?style=flat-square) <sub>#15 · line 4/10</sub>  
**太好了！我们可以做番茄炒蛋。**  
*Tài hǎo le! Wǒmen kěyǐ zuò fānqié chǎodàn.*  
💬 Great! We can make scrambled eggs with tomatoes.

💚 ![Riduan](https://img.shields.io/badge/Riduan-%E7%A4%BC%E7%AB%AF-0c9c86?style=flat-square) <sub>#16 · line 4/10</sub>  
**我不会做饭，可是我会切菜！**  
*Wǒ bú huì zuòfàn, kěshì wǒ huì qiē cài!*  
💬 I can't cook, but I can chop vegetables!

🧡 ![Lin Feifei](https://img.shields.io/badge/Lin_Feifei-%E6%9E%97%E8%8F%B2%E8%8F%B2-f08c00?style=flat-square) <sub>#17 · line 6/10</sub>  
**哈哈，礼端，你上次切到手了！**  
*Hāhā, Lǐduān, nǐ shàng cì qiēdào shǒu le!*  
💬 Haha, Riduan — last time you cut your hand!

💚 ![Riduan](https://img.shields.io/badge/Riduan-%E7%A4%BC%E7%AB%AF-0c9c86?style=flat-square) <sub>#18 · line 5/10</sub>  
**不好意思，那是我第一次嘛！**  
*Bù hǎoyìsi, nà shì wǒ dì-yī cì ma!*  
💬 Sorry — that was my first time!

💙 ![Jason](https://img.shields.io/badge/Jason-%E6%9D%B0%E6%A3%AE-3b5bdb?style=flat-square) <sub>#19 · line 4/10</sub>  
**做饭要多久？我饿了。**  
*Zuòfàn yào duōjiǔ? Wǒ è le.*  
💬 How long will the cooking take? I'm hungry.

🩷 ![Jamilah](https://img.shields.io/badge/Jamilah-%E4%BD%B3%E7%B1%B3%E6%8B%89-d6336c?style=flat-square) <sub>#20 · line 5/10</sub>  
**大概二十分钟。请坐，等一下。**  
*Dàgài èrshí fēnzhōng. Qǐng zuò, děng yíxià.*  
💬 About twenty minutes. Have a seat and wait a moment.


### Act 3 · Let's Go on Holiday! 🏝️

## 🎤 How to Practise

1. **Pick a character.** Each person takes one colour.
2. **Listen first.** Open the [interactive version](https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/mandarin-script/) and press 🔊 next to any line, or 🐢 to hear it slowly.
3. **Practice mode.** Choose *“Practice as…”* in the toolbar and your lines get blurred. Press **▶ Play whole scene** and it pauses on your lines so you can say them yourself.
4. **Swap roles** and go again! 🔁

> 🔊 **Best voice:** open the page in **Microsoft Edge** to get the natural *Xiaoxiao* voice. Chrome and Safari also work.
